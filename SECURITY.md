# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue, discussion, or pull request for security problems.

Email your report to **support@roolink.io** with "Security" in the subject line.

Please include:

- the affected repository, package, and version
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- the impact you think it has

## What to expect

- We'll confirm we received your report within **3 business days**.
- We'll send you a first assessment within **7 days**.
- We'll keep you updated until it's fixed, and credit you in the advisory
  unless you'd rather stay anonymous.

## Scope

In scope:

- [roolink-go](https://github.com/roolinkio/roolink-go)
- [roolink-js](https://github.com/roolinkio/roolink-js)
- [roolink-py](https://github.com/roolinkio/roolink-py)
- [tlsprofiles](https://github.com/roolinkio/tlsprofiles)
- the RooLink API

Out of scope:

- denial-of-service and load testing
- social engineering
- testing against accounts or data that aren't yours

## Supported versions

We fix security issues in the **latest major release** of each SDK. Please
upgrade before reporting if you can.
